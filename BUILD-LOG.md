- **XP-remaining calibration:** per-course size = median over students with 5–95% progress of `xpRemaining / (1 − progress)` from the Math Academy API; saved to `reference/course-xp-size.json`. Applied to Timeback's `pctCompleteApp`, the estimate was within 25% of Math Academy's figure for about two thirds of students. Timeback's own `course.metadata.metrics.totalXp` under-estimates the Math Academy course size for every course measured, so it is documented as a lower bound only.

Hence example 7's heuristic framing and its pairing with the quiet check. Map saved to `reference/topic-course-map.json`.
